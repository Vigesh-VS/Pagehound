# Aurora X2 Air Purifier User Manual

## Safety Instructions

Read all instructions before using the purifier. Place the unit on a firm, level surface at least 30 cm away from walls and furniture so that air can flow freely around it.

Do not use the purifier in bathrooms or other places with high humidity, and never spray water or cleaning liquids directly onto the unit. Unplug the power adapter before cleaning, moving or replacing the filter.

Keep the purifier away from children younger than eight unless they are supervised, and never insert fingers or objects into the air outlet.

## Package Contents

The box contains the Aurora X2 main unit, a three-layer HEPA filter already installed, a 24 V power adapter, a quick-start card and this manual. Keep the packaging for at least the warranty period in case the unit needs to be returned for service.

## Getting Started

Remove the plastic wrap from the filter before first use; the purifier will not start while the wrap is still on. Connect the power adapter to the socket at the back of the unit and press the power button for two seconds.

To connect the purifier to the mobile app, hold the Wi-Fi button for five seconds until the indicator blinks blue. Open the app, choose Add Device and follow the on-screen pairing steps. The purifier supports 2.4 GHz networks only.

## Operating Modes

Auto mode reads the built-in laser particle sensor and adjusts the fan speed automatically. The ring light shows air quality: blue means good, yellow means moderate and red means poor.

Sleep mode dims all lights and runs the fan at its lowest speed, producing about 22 dB of noise, which is quieter than a whisper. Turbo mode runs the fan at full speed for 15 minutes and then returns to Auto.

The child lock disables all buttons. To turn it on or off, press and hold the mode and timer buttons together for three seconds.

## Filter Replacement

The filter should be replaced roughly every 6 to 8 months, depending on air quality and daily usage. When the filter life drops below 5 percent, the filter indicator turns red.

Use only the genuine replacement filter, part number AX2-HEPA-13. To replace it, unplug the unit, turn it upside down, twist the bottom cover anticlockwise and lift out the old filter. Insert the new filter with the pull tab facing up and close the cover.

After fitting a new filter, reset the filter counter by holding the filter button for seven seconds until you hear a beep.

## Cleaning and Maintenance

Wipe the outer shell with a soft dry cloth once a week. Vacuum the pre-filter mesh every two weeks to remove pet hair and large dust particles, which extends the life of the main filter.

The particle sensor lens should be cleaned every two months with a cotton swab. The sensor cover is located on the left side of the unit behind a small sliding door.

## Error Codes

E1 means the filter is missing or not seated correctly. Reinstall the filter and make sure the bottom cover is fully locked.

E2 indicates a fan motor fault. Turn the purifier off for ten minutes and restart it; if the code returns, contact service.

E3 shows that the particle sensor is blocked or dirty. Clean the sensor lens as described in the maintenance section.

E4 is a Wi-Fi connection failure. Check that your router uses the 2.4 GHz band and that the password was entered correctly.

E5 appears when the unit overheats because the air intake is blocked. Move the purifier away from curtains and walls and let it cool down.

E6 is a firmware update failure. Keep the unit connected to Wi-Fi and it will retry the update automatically within 24 hours.

## Technical Specifications

Clean air delivery rate is 380 cubic metres per hour, suitable for rooms up to 45 square metres. Power consumption ranges from 3 W in Sleep mode to 38 W in Turbo mode. The unit weighs 5.8 kg and measures 26 by 26 by 58 cm.

## Warranty

The Aurora X2 comes with a two-year limited warranty from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship. Filters are consumables and are not covered.

The warranty is void if the unit has been opened, repaired by an unauthorised person or damaged by liquid. To make a claim, register the product within 30 days and keep the original invoice.
