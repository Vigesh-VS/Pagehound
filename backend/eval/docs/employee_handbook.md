# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## 1. Working Hours

Our core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside these hours, people may arrange their day as they prefer, provided they complete a standard 40-hour week. Team leads publish a weekly rota so that each product squad always has one engineer on call during business hours.

Overtime must be approved in advance by a team lead. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate, or it can be taken as time off in lieu within the following quarter.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 2. Leave and Time Off

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, which accrue at two days per month. Up to 8 unused days may be carried into the next year; any balance above that lapses on 31 March.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave each year. A medical certificate is required only when an absence lasts longer than three consecutive working days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks at full pay for the secondary caregiver. It can be started up to two weeks before the expected date of birth or adoption.

To request any kind of leave, submit form HR-17B in the people portal at least ten working days in advance, except for sick leave, which is reported on the same day.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 3. Remote Work

Employees may work remotely for up to three days a week after completing their probation period. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head and are reviewed every six months.

Remote workers receive a one-time home office allowance of Rs 25,000 for a chair, desk or monitor, and a monthly internet stipend of Rs 1,500. Receipts must be uploaded within 30 days of purchase.

When working from a public place, employees must use the company VPN and must never leave a laptop unattended, even briefly.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 4. Travel and Expenses

Business travel must be booked through the approved travel desk. Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

The daily meal allowance during domestic travel is Rs 1,200 and during international travel is USD 60. Hotel bookings should not exceed the city cap published by finance, which is Rs 6,500 per night for metro cities.

Expense claims are filed under policy number EXP-204 and must be submitted within 45 days of the expense date. Claims older than 45 days are rejected automatically by the finance system.

Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are never reimbursable.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 5. Information Security

All laptops use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are managed through the company password manager.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control and the cloud console. Hardware security keys are issued to anyone with production access.

Suspected phishing emails should be reported with the Report Phish button, and any lost or stolen device must be reported to the security desk within one hour by calling extension 4410.

Customer data may only be stored in approved systems. Copying customer records to personal devices or personal cloud storage is a dismissible offence.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 6. Onboarding

New joiners receive their laptop on the first day and complete a five-day onboarding programme. The programme covers company history, product walkthroughs, security training and a pairing session with a buddy from their team.

Each new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy who checks in weekly for the first 90 days. Probation lasts six months, after which the manager holds a confirmation review.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 7. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review combines a self-assessment, feedback from at least three peers and a manager assessment.

Ratings use a five-point scale. Employees rated 4 or 5 in two consecutive cycles are eligible for an out-of-cycle promotion discussion. Salary revisions take effect from the first of July.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.

## 8. Learning Budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of Rs 40,000 for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Requests above Rs 15,000 for a single item need approval from a team lead.

Employees can also take up to five paid study days a year for exams or certifications related to their role.

This policy is reviewed every year by the People Operations team and updated when local regulations change.
